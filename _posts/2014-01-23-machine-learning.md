> A computer program that learns from experience and becomes better at performing a task.

## Supervised Learning

Program is fed "right answers", sample data and it learns to compute more "right answers".

### Regression
Continuous Valued Output, for example, determine the price of a given house.

### Classification
Discrete Valued Output, for example, classify if a tumor is malignant or benign, or whether a customer account is hacked or not.
