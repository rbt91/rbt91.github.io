> A computer program that learns from experience and becomes better at performing a task.

## Supervised Learning

Program is fed a dataset with known "right answers" and it learns to compute more "right answers". For example, dataset may provide prices for houses in area A with respect to size of house. A machine learning algorithm can analyze this data to determine the price of a house not in the original dataset.

### Regression
Continuous Valued Output, for example, determine the price of a given house.

### Classification
Discrete Valued Output, for example, classify if a tumor is malignant or benign, or whether a customer account is hacked. This is also how GMail categorizes emails as spam.

## Unsupervised Learning

A dataset is given but we dont know what are the "right answers" - there are no labels on data.

### Clustering algorithm
The way Google News groups different news items into one cohesive story.
